# Glossary

**ε_c1, ε_r1, ε_r2** — Normalized displacement parameters from azimuth velocity (v_c/V), range velocity (v_r/V), and range acceleration (a_r R0/V²). (Ch 11)

**Azimuth smearing** — Focusing artifact from target azimuth velocity or range acceleration; one of three motion tells. (Ch 11)

**B_shift** — Fixed azimuth-frequency separation held between master and slave sub-bands; selects the observed vibrational frequency. Higher B_shift → lower observed mechanical frequency. (Ch 11)

**Big Void** — Tag #19; a large parallelepiped-shaped internal structure connected to the Zed complex via a double horizontal connection. (Ch 7)

**Cap / plug (Vesuvius)** — Vibrational-energy singularity below the main crater, read as denser material (cooled compact lava) blocking the conduit. (Ch 14)

**Category X (ISR)** — Search-report mark meaning the cited document alone destroys novelty or inventive step; applied to all 10 claims of WO 2024/008365. (Ch 10, Ch 16)

**Coherence map** — Quality/reliability measure of an interferometric SAR pair; values near 1 indicate low noise. (Ch 5)

**Complex structure** — Catalog category for multi-part discovered features (tags #9, #10) that aren't simple single rooms/corridors. (Ch 7)

**CSAMT / magnetotelluric tomography** — Independent electrical-sounding modality used as the cross-modality structural reference at Vesuvius. (Ch 15)

**Cubic restoring force** — Nonlinear spring term (eq. 15) that dominates when L ≈ L0; yields a Duffing-type oscillator. (Ch 12, Ch 14)

**DFT2 / FFT2** — Two-dimensional (fast) Fourier transform; block 2 of the computational scheme, computed once per image. (Ch 11, Ch 13)

**Doppler centroid anomaly** — Deviation in the Doppler centroid frequency during azimuth focusing, exploited as the carrier of vibration information. (Ch 1, Ch 3)

**Doppler sub-aperture** — A narrower-bandwidth slice of the full synthesized Doppler bandwidth, used as one "look" in the multi-temporal vibration-tracking strategy. (Ch 3, Ch 11)

**Duffing oscillator** — Damped, forced nonlinear oscillator r̈ + λṙ + ω²(1+ξr²)r = f(ωt); the patent's true vibration model before linearization. (Ch 12)

**Elastic constant (ξ)** — Spring stiffness in the surface spring-lattice model; also the nonlinearity coefficient in eq. (17). (Ch 12)

**Front Corridor** — Tag #18; corridor located just behind the external V-shaped entrance structure; proposed drilling target for physical validation. (Ch 7, Ch 9)

**Geocoding / in-depth geolocation** — Block 11; places the focused tomogram into a 3-D geographic reference system for GIS release. (Ch 13)

**GIS standard output** — Claim 10; the tomographic extrapolation is delivered at any known Geographical Information System standard. (Ch 10, Ch 13)

**Grand Gallery** — Known large corridor connecting the King's and Queen's Chambers. (Ch 2)

**Grotto** — Known void feature detected with the Zed/King's Chamber tomography; connects toward the unfinished room. (Ch 6)

**Guard band (B_DL = B_cD/2)** — Bandwidth deliberately excluded from the SAR matched filter to preserve sensitivity to target motion; the room in which the band pair slides. (Ch 3, Ch 11)

**Harmonic-oscillator vibrational model** — Model treating each tomographic-line pixel as a damped mass-spring system whose measured amplitude/phase feed the tomographic inversion. (Ch 4, Ch 12)

**INGV in-situ stations (IV-VCRE, IV-VBKN)** — The two Vesuvius seismographs both radar-visible and inside the SAR scene; ground truth for the vibration validation. (Ch 15)

**InSAR (Interferometric SAR)** — Repeat-pass SAR technique using two acquisitions to compute a phase/coherence map; used here for the external 8-facet finding. (Ch 5)

**International Search Report (ISR)** — EPO search opinion attached to a PCT publication; for WO 2024/008365 it cites one document, the inventor's own arXiv preprint, as X against claims 1–10. (Ch 10)

**Investigation frequency** — The mechanical frequency the tomogram is tuned to; sets the sound wavelength and therefore the depth/resolution trade. 200 Hz (Vesuvius) / 12.5 kHz (Giza) / ~22 kHz (patent). (Ch 12, Ch 14)

**K_z** — Elevation wavenumber, 4πB⊥/(λ r_i sin θ); carries the orthogonal baseline into the depth phase ramp of the steering matrix. (Ch 12)

**King's Chamber** — Known chamber containing the sarcophagus, below the Zed, off-axis to the south. (Ch 2)

**L, L0** — Spring length at maximum tension and at rest (no mass); their ratio governs whether linearization is valid. (Ch 12)

**Layover vs. foreshortening** — Opposing SAR geometric distortions; the Vesuvius stress test showed the layover side reconstructs measurably better. (Ch 14)

**Little Void** — Tag #17; ill-defined void behind the pyramid's original entrance. (Ch 7)

**Longitudinal vs transverse oscillation** — Tension-domain (longitudinal) oscillations propagate at roughly twice the transverse frequency; their coupling is nonlinear. (Ch 12)

**Master / slave images** — The two sub-band refocusings of the *same* SAR image, held B_shift apart, whose sub-pixel disparity is the vibration measurement. (Ch 11, Ch 13)

**Metric determination** — Estimating physical dimensions of discovered structures using the pyramid's known base length as an internal calibration reference. (Ch 7)

**Micro-Doppler effect** — Extra Doppler shift from target micro-movement (vibration/rotation), classically a SAR imaging artifact, repurposed here as signal. (Ch 1)

**Micro-Motion (MM) Doppler Tomography** — The core method: extracting vibration signatures from Doppler sub-apertures of a single SAR image and inverting them tomographically. (Ch 1, Ch 3, Ch 4, Ch 10, Ch 14)

**Multi-Chromatic Analysis (MCA)** — Range-direction technique for retrieving unambiguous height/phase information, applied here in the Doppler direction for vibration extraction. (Ch 1, Ch 3, Ch 14)

**N_D** — Number of Doppler sub-aperture refocused images; *is* the digital sampling rate of the mechanical wave being observed. (Ch 11)

**Non-resonating material** — Detection signature of vent conduits: absence of vibrational energy rather than presence. (Ch 14)

**Northern Underground Complex Structure** — Tag #9; multi-part structure below the pyramid's north base, geometrically similar to structures at Zawyet El-Aryan and Sekhemkhet pyramids. (Ch 7)

**Orthogonal baseline (B⊥, B_i)** — Per-sub-aperture geometric baseline used in the tomographic steering matrix. (Ch 4, Ch 12)

**Persistent Scatterer Interferometry (PSI)** — Prior-generation SAR structural-health-monitoring technique the MM method builds on. (Ch 1)

**Phonon** — Quantum of vibrational/acoustic energy; the patent frames the method as photon-versus-phonon interaction. (Ch 10, Ch 16)

**Pixel/sub-pixel tracking (coregistration)** — Core measurement operation estimating displacement of a pixel between master/slave sub-aperture realizations; block 7. (Ch 3, Ch 11, Ch 13)

**PRIME AI template residue** — Section 2.1 and equation (1) of arXiv:2206.09200v2 are unremoved LaTeX lorem-ipsum filler, not part of the method. (Ch 16)

**Pulse compression** — The focusing operation h(z) = A†Y; makes structureless raw vibration traces legible as a tomogram. (Ch 12, Ch 13)

**Queen's Bottom Room** — Tag #14; structure below the Queen's Chamber connected via a small conduit. (Ch 7)

**Queen's Chamber** — Known on-axis chamber below the King's Chamber. (Ch 2)

**Range-walking** — Range defocusing spreading backscattered energy over several range cells in the presence of target range speed. (Ch 11)

**Rectangular spectrum** — The 2-D DFT of a focused SLC point target is rectangular (eq. 3); this is what licenses clean sub-banding. (Ch 11)

**SARPROZ** — Third-party SAR processing software (D. Perissin) credited for supporting calculations; not the tomographic engine, which is the authors' own code. (Ch 16)

**Seismic perpetual ripple** — The Earth's always-present ambient mechanical field, plus man-made vibration; the method's illumination source. (Ch 10)

**SLC (Single-Look-Complex)** — Focused, complex-valued SAR image format; the sole raw input needed per tomogram (no image pair required). The patent also accepts raw-configuration data. (Ch 3, Ch 10, Ch 13)

**Southern Corridor** — Tag #3; connects the eastern/western ascending ramps at ~90 m height. (Ch 7)

**Space-Sonar** — The authors' framing of the invention: sonar-like sounding performed from orbit via electromagnetic-wave/matter interaction. (Ch 10)

**Spring-lattice surface model** — The Earth's surface as a field of perpetually oscillating spring-mass elements, one contribution per image pixel. (Ch 12)

**SRTM DEM** — Shuttle Radar Topography Mission elevation model; the independent reference for topographic validation of a tomogram's surface. (Ch 15)

**Standing wave** — Superposition of an impulse-launched wave and its reflection; the premise for treating the surface as continuously ringing. (Ch 12)

**Steering matrix A(K_z, z)** — Matrix encoding phase-vs-elevation information per baseline; the best approximation of a DFT operator on Y. (Ch 4, Ch 12)

**Stop-and-go approximation** — Standard SAR imaging assumption (target static during acquisition) whose breakdown under vibration produces the exploitable artifacts this method measures. (Ch 1, Ch 11)

**Three-layer validation** — Validating geometry (DEM), measurement (in-situ seismographs), and structure (cross-modality) separately, because each can fail independently. (Ch 15)

**Tomographic filter** — Block 11 stage applied to the focused tomogram before geolocation. (Ch 13)

**Tomographic line** — A chosen line of contiguous pixels from surface to depth along which vibration-based inversion is performed; orientation determines which internal features become visible. (Ch 4, Ch 6, Ch 13, Ch 14)

**Tomographic map** — The focused tomographic image (block 10); the output of pulse compression on the raw vibrational data. (Ch 13)

**Tomographic resolution (δ_z = λR/2A)** — Depth resolution formula, where λ is the **sound** wavelength in the medium, not the radar wavelength. ≈36 m (Vesuvius) / ≈0.92 m (Giza paper) / ≈1.30 m (patent). (Ch 4, Ch 12, Ch 14)

**Unfinished Room** — Known off-axis, underground chamber; detected only weakly by the tomography. (Ch 2, Ch 6)

**Vent aperture** — Candidate lava path at Vesuvius; 11 catalogued on the western/external side, 5 inside the main crater. (Ch 14)

**XP091253966** — EPO citation code for arXiv:2206.09200 v1 (18 June 2022), the sole document cited as category X against WO 2024/008365. (Ch 10, Ch 16)

**Zed** — Large granite monument above the King's Chamber (angled roof + five parallel slabs); treated by the authors as a distinct acoustic/structural element. (Ch 2)

**Zed Complex Structure** — Tag #10; square structure developing around the Zed near Davison's Chamber height. (Ch 7)
