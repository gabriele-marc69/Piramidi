3. SUMMARY

Micro-Motion (MM) SAR Doppler tomography (Biondi & Malanga, Remote Sensing
2022, 14, 5231) claims depth-resolved imaging of the interior of large static
structures from a single SAR acquisition. The method is potentially
transformative for non-invasive structural assessment, but no independent,
fully reproducible verification exists. We built an open-source end-to-end
pipeline and applied it to free Sentinel-1 IW SLC stacks over the Giza
pyramids, establishing a quantitative information budget which shows that IW
TOPS data are intrinsically insufficient:

| Quantity                             | Value  |
| ------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Independent Doppler looks per target | ~12    |
| Nominal depth resolution (dz)        | ~1.2 m |
| Ambiguity height (z_amb)             | ~8.5 m |
| Resolvable depth cells               | ~7     |
| Structure scale to resolve           | ~140 m |

We therefore request high-dwell Spotlight (preferred) or Stripmap SLC
acquisitions over the Giza plateau to (a) validate the MM tomographic chain
against the KNOWN internal structures of the Great Pyramid, and (b) publish
the first independent, fully open assessment of the method's real
capabilities and limits. We deliberately do not frame the project as a search
for undiscovered chambers: a negative result is equally publishable. All
code, parameters, and derived products will be released openly.
